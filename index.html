Admissions & EAMCET Information

Admission Process

1. Appear for AP EAPCET (EAMCET).

2. Check your rank and eligibility.

3. Complete counselling registration.

4. Select Vignan Lara and your preferred course.

5. Check seat allotment and reporting instructions.

Required EAMCET Rank

Required rank depends on the course, category, counselling round and admission year.

Please check the official counselling website for the latest cutoff ranks.
